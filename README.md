# Airline Customer Satisfaction & Operational Optimization

## Executive Summary
This project analyzes passenger process ratings to identify operational bottlenecks and improve service delivery within the airport station. By cleaning and evaluating customer touchpoints alongside departure delays, this analysis provides data-driven recommendations exactly on the process that need improvements to maximize passenger loyalty and on-time performance.

---

## Key Business Insights (TL;DR)
* **Process Bottlenecks:** Though Baggage handling did great, however it received the lowest average satisfaction scores.
* **Departure Status Impact:** Discovered that onetime departure stood at 67% which was achieved by seamless check-in service in the check-in counter.
* **Station Optimization:** Segmented performance metrics by individual airport stations, revealing that specific regional hubs require targeted staff reallocation to resolve localized check-in delays.

---

## The Technical Approach

### 1. Data Refinement & Cleaning
To ensure accurate analysis, the raw customer dataset underwent rigorous cleaning:
* **Handling Missing Values:** Resolved missing data fields in passenger satisfaction scores and flight delay durations to prevent analytical bias.
* **Feature Engineering:** Grouped granular numerical ratings into categorical satisfaction tiers (Low, Medium, High) to identify critical improvement areas easily.
* **Data Standardization:** Normalized station codes and flight departure statuses to establish uniform reporting criteria across all locations.

### 2. Tools & Skills Utilized
* **Data Manipulation:** SQL (CTEs, Window Functions)` for aggregating process scores.
* **Exploratory Analysis:** Statistical breakdown of passenger demographics against satisfaction benchmarks.
* **Data Visualization:** Built executive-ready matrices and trend charts to map station-specific performance.

---

## Visual Storytelling
> **Executive Recommendation:** To protect brand loyalty, resources must be reallocated to optimize station check-in efficiency and minimize controllable departure delays, as these represent the primary drivers of negative customer sentiment.
